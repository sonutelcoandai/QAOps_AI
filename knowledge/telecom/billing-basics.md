# Billing Basics

Billing is the process of calculating and generating charges for telecom services consumed by customers.

Key Concepts:

- Billing Account
- Invoice
- Rating
- Charging
- Usage Records
- Bill Cycle

Common Validation Areas:

- Invoice Accuracy
- Charge Calculation
- Tax Validation
- Discount Validation
- Revenue Assurance

Risks:

- Incorrect Billing
- Lost Revenue
- Duplicate Charges
- Missing Charges
